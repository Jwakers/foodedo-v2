# Research

Scratch space for product and UX notes: interviews, competitor observations, import-source behaviour, cooking-in-the-kitchen constraints.

Notes here are **not** policy. When a finding is durable, graduate it into `knowledge/principles/`, `knowledge/product/`, or `knowledge/ux/` and link back if the evidence matters.

Do not treat research files as a reason to add features that fail the product feature test.
