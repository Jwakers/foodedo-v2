# Vision

Foodedo is **a personal food decision engine** that turns saved food, preferences, and context into useful decisions.

Recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, favourites, imports, discovery, and recommendations are **capabilities**, not the thesis. They exist only insofar as they help someone decide what to eat and then make it happen.

## Positioning

People already have recipes. They drown in them. The product is not a better recipe box, a weekly planner, or a content feed. It is the layer that answers “what are we eating?” using what the person already cares about.

Optimise for habitual use. Success is when opening Foodedo is the natural response to that question — not when the user has a complete catalogue or a perfect week on a calendar.

## What we are not

- A social network for food
- A publisher CMS with an app bolted on
- A household operating system as a prerequisite
- A meal-plan generator that requires a week of configuration
- A discovery marketplace that ignores the user's own saved food
